# App icon brief (for an AI image tool or an artist)

Reference picture: `icon-reference.png` in this folder. It shows the idea, not the finished look.

## What we want
**Main icon: a steam engine arriving at night, seen from straight in front.**
- An 1860s–1880s British-style steam engine coming towards the viewer on straight track.
- Dark smokebox door in the middle with a brass number plate ("1830"). One bright lamp at the top.
- Red buffer beam with two buffers. Buffers look like real railway buffers: flat round plates on short housings.
- No cow-catcher or plough.
- Thick white-grey steam rising from the chimney and drifting back over the top.
- Warm glow on the horizon, wet rails catching the lamp light.
- Dark teal and black night, with warm brass and red accents. Game colours: deep green `#163d2c`, brass `#c9a23a`, red `#b8362a`.
- **Style:** realistic and painterly, like a good mobile strategy game icon. Not cartoon, not a photo.

**Second piece, the crest (optional):** the same engine inside a brass shield, with a red ribbon banner across the bottom reading **IRON & STEAM**. This is used for the store banner and the loading screen, not the phone icon.

## Rules for the phone icon
- **No text** on the phone icon. It's too small to read.
- Engine big and centred. It has to read at 48 px, so make one strong clear shape with few tiny details.
- Keep everything important inside the middle **66%** of the square. Android crops icons to circles and rounded squares.
- Make it square. Background fills the whole square: no transparency, no rounded corners, no drop shadow around the edge.

## What to send back
1. `icon-1024.png`, 1024 × 1024, the full icon.
2. If possible, the same picture as two layers: the **background** (sky, glow, track) and the **foreground** (engine and steam on a transparent background). Android uses the layers for its animated icons. If not, the single picture is fine; I'll make the layers from it.
3. Optional: the crest picture, 1024 × 1024 or larger.

I'll then make all the Android icon sizes, the 512 px store icon and the store banner.

## Example prompt for an AI image tool
> Mobile game app icon, square, realistic painterly style. A Victorian British steam locomotive seen from straight in front, arriving at night on straight railway track towards the viewer. Black smokebox door with brass number plate, one bright headlamp, red buffer beam with two flat round buffers, no cow-catcher. Thick white steam rising from the chimney. Dark teal night sky, warm amber glow on the horizon, wet rails reflecting the lamp. Deep green, brass and red colour palette. Engine large and centred, strong silhouette, no text, no border.

If the tool adds a cow-catcher or text, add: "no cow-catcher, no pilot, no text, no letters".
