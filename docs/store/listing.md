## Full description (max 4000)
Iron & Steam is a railway-building game for your phone or tablet. Lay track between towns, mines, farms and factories, buy trains, and earn money from the passengers, mail and freight they carry. Start in the 1830s with a small steam engine, and grow your railroad as the decades bring faster, stronger locomotives, then diesel and electric traction.

It plays alone and at your own pace: no competitors, no stock market, no timers to beat. You can pause at any time, and the game saves itself as you play.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Drag to build track, tap to place stations, and tap a station to buy trains and set their stops.
• Towns grow where your trains call. Factories, mines, farms and mills need their goods moved, and ask for others in return.
• Watch each station's supply and demand, see what your lines earn, and fix the ones that don't.
• Borrow from the bank to expand, then pay it back. Fuel, wages, track upkeep and taxes all count.
• Bridges, hills and rivers make some routes costly. Choose your line with care.

REAL MAPS AND RANDOM MAPS
• Play on real regions: the Eastern United States, Great Britain, Central Europe and the Alps, and the American West.
• Or generate a random map. Pick the size, the water and the roughness, and the start year.
• Three difficulty levels. Scenarios give you a fixed goal and a star rating.
• The years matter: engines, wagons and track improvements arrive on their real dates, and economic booms and busts come and go.

WHAT YOU GET
• Over twenty historical locomotives and many types of wagons.
• Goals, achievements, a yearly report with the whole year's accounts, and a history of your railroad.
• Drawn entirely in code, in a calm, poster-like style. Works in landscape on phones and tablets.
• No ads. No in-app purchases. No account. No network use. Your saves stay on your device.

Iron & Steam is made by one developer. Bug reports and ideas are welcome by e-mail.
