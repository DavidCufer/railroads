# Privacy policy — Iron & Steam

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Iron & Steam ("the app") is a single-player game made by David Cufer ("the developer").

**The app collects no personal data.** It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no
in-app purchases. It makes no network requests: it never sends anything off your device. It asks for no sensitive permissions (no
location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or advertising ID). The only permission in its manifest is Android's
standard "internet" permission that the app framework (Capacitor) includes by default; the game does not use it.

**What stays on your device.** Your saved games, settings (units, sound, display) and the list of hints and
achievements you have seen are stored locally by the app, in the app's own storage. The developer cannot see them.
Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes them.

**Children.** The app is meant for ages 13 and up. Because it collects nothing, it holds no data about anyone of any age.

**Changes.** If this ever changes (for example, if a later version adds an online feature), this policy will be
updated first and the change described in the release notes.
